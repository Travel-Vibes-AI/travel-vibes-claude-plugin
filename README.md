# Travel Vibes: Trip Itineraries

Travel Vibes helps you plan trips by turning your preferences into full itineraries. Get a day-by-day plan with flights, accommodation, activities, budget, maps, and more. Save and edit your itinerary in Scratchpad, and share it with travel buddies. No forms or rigid templates, just describe your vibe and get a ready-to-use plan.

## Use it

Ask Claude to plan a trip, even with just a destination:

- "Plan a relaxed 3-day Lisbon trip"
- "Build a Tokyo trip with daily plans"
- "Plan a food-first trip to Mexico City"

Claude writes the itinerary and shows it in the Travel Vibes widget right in the chat. Answer its question or ask for a change, and the same trip updates. Tap "Open in Scratchpad" to continue in Travel Vibes: verified places with ratings and photos, an interactive map, weather for your dates, hotel and flight search, sharing, and PDF or image export.

The plugin brings two things: the Travel Vibes connector, which you add from the plugin's Connectors tab (no account or sign-in needed), and a skill that tells Claude when to use it and how to keep the planning moving.

## Data

The plugin sends the itinerary Claude writes for your trip (the destination, the dates you mention and the plan itself) to Travel Vibes at gpt.travelvibes.ai, which stores it so the widget can show it and you can open, edit and share the trip. When a trip is changed, the previous version is kept for 30 days. The plugin sends nothing else and runs no code on your computer.
